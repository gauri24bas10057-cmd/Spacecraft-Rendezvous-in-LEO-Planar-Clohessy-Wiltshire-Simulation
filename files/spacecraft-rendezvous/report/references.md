## Project assumptions and results (not references)

Constants R_E = 6378 km (rounded; WGS-84 equatorial radius is 6378.137 km) and
mu = 398600.4418 km^3/s^2 are standard textbook values. All numerical results, tables and figures are
produced by this repository's code; the assumptions are listed in `report.md`, Section 6.
